Privacy Policy

Aura Pics — by Bulletplay Studios

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bulletplay Studios (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the Aura Pics mobile application (“the App”).

1. Accounts and information you provide

Aura Pics does not require you to create an account or sign in. We do not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise location to use the App. If you contact us for support, we will receive the information you choose to include in your message.

2. App preferences stored on your device

The App may store preferences and app activity on your device, such as favorites, prompts you have unlocked, and related app state. This information is used to provide app features and is not an account profile. Removing app data or uninstalling the App may remove locally stored information.

When you choose to copy a prompt, the prompt text is placed on your device’s clipboard so you can paste it elsewhere. If you choose to open or use another AI service, that service’s own privacy policy and terms apply to your use of it.

3. Prompt content and images hosted by Supabase

The App connects to Supabase to retrieve prompt information and associated images. Supabase processes requests needed to deliver this content and operates under its own privacy and security practices. Content and app data hosted in our Supabase project may be processed by Supabase’s infrastructure. We configure access controls for the project and do not intend the public App to expose administrative credentials.

4. Advertising and Google AdMob

Aura Pics may display rewarded advertisements through Google AdMob. When ads are requested or shown, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as IP address, device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions, approximate region inferred from network information, diagnostics, and fraud-prevention signals. The information processed can depend on your device, consent choices, regional requirements, and Google’s services and settings. Google may use information to provide, measure, and protect advertising, and where permitted, personalize ads.

We do not directly receive your Google account password through AdMob. For more information about how Google handles information, review Google’s Privacy Policy.

5. How information is used

Information processed in connection with the App is used to deliver prompts and images, remember local preferences and unlocks, provide rewarded ads, maintain app functionality, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot technical problems.

6. Sharing and third-party services

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that support the App, including Supabase for content delivery and Google AdMob for advertising. These providers may process information according to their own policies and the services they provide. Information may also be disclosed if required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the App, its users, or our rights.

7. Data retention and security

Local app data remains on your device until you remove it or uninstall the App, subject to your device’s behavior. Information processed by third-party providers is retained according to the relevant provider’s policies and settings. We use reasonable measures intended to protect app content and systems, but no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children’s privacy

Aura Pics is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through a support message, contact us so we can review the request. Advertising availability and treatment may vary by age and region.

9. Your choices

You can stop using the App or uninstall it at any time. You can manage advertising or privacy choices through available device settings and any consent options presented in the App. Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights. Contact us to ask about a privacy request.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App or its services change. We will post the updated version on this page and revise the “Last updated” date.

11. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, contact Bulletplay Studios at: bulletplaystudios@gmail.com.

This policy should be reviewed and updated if Aura Pics’ data practices, advertising SDK configuration, or third-party services change.
